This template is to be used for all GTAC submissions (and is largely identical to the versions from
previous GTACs). This document provides the raw Word file to be used, and we also provide some

The GTAC papers are meant to be “archival” manuscripts just like any external conference or journal
paper. This means that the paper should be self-contained such that if an AMD engineer were to read
this paper several years from now, the paper would still make sense without the reader having to
understand the full technological context of the present day. As such, the introduction should
provide some context and background to help such a future reader better understand the challenges
and problems that we are trying to solve now with your work. However, as these papers will all be
restricted for AMD-internal usage only, you may assume that the reader has a certain familiarity
with our general business and technology areas. You do not need to (nor do you have the space) to
provide an in-depth tutorial on, e.g., the principles of caching, the basic operations of
field-effect transistors, or a history of neural networks starting at the original perceptron from
1958.

\section{Conclusions}
The conclusions section should state key conclusions and insights.  While perhaps not entirely
“conclusive”, this is also a good place to discuss any limitations of the work covered in this paper
(e.g., “we focused on the DL1 cache but have not yet extended the analysis to the L2”), speculations
for future directions (e.g., “the results were promising for machine learning workloads, and due to
our matrix-oriented optimizations we expect our technique to have some potential for scientific
computing”), and any commentary on potential, planned, or actual impact on AMD (e.g., hardware,
software, tools, methodologies, roadmap).  The last part is useful to provide the reviewers with
additional context on the broader value of the work.  We recommend simply cut-and-pasting or
re-summarizing the abstract as that does not really add any value to the paper.
